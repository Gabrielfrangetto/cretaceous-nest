Equipment and scar anchors

Pick what to place: equipment (worn by adults only, so fitted on the adult sprites) or scars (on every stage: baby, juvenile and adult each have their own drawing). Click or drag anywhere on the dinosaur to move the highlighted one there, then size and turn it. The deep look and the superficial one each have their own place: what “Shown as” shows is the one you move. Scars start with none on the drawing: add the one chosen, and click any added scar to pick it. On an animated drawing scars and equipment move with the skin under them by themselves (Play to see it); to steer one yourself, stop on a frame and move it there: that frame becomes a key. Changes are kept in this browser; copy the result into js/gear-art.js (or send it to Claude).
