Equipment and scar anchors

Pick what to place: equipment (worn by adults only, so fitted on the adult sprites) or scars (on every stage: baby, juvenile and adult each have their own drawing). Click or drag anywhere on the dinosaur to move the highlighted one there, then size and turn it. Scars start with none on the drawing: add the one chosen, and click any added scar to pick it. An animated drawing has its frames: stop on a frame and move a scar there to give it a key, and between keys it moves along by itself; or let it follow the body on its own, then mend the frames it misses. Changes are kept in this browser; copy the result into js/gear-art.js (or send it to Claude).
