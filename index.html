Cretaceous Nest

Hatch dinosaurs, raise them from tiny babies to towering adults, and lead them into the wild land around your nest.

See how it works

1 · Hatch

Your first dinosaur is waiting

Pick one of four starter babies, two meat eaters and two plant eaters. Every egg after that is found out in the world or laid by your own dinosaurs, and every one hides different genes.

2 · Raise

Feed them and watch them grow

Babies get hungry on their own clock, even while the page is closed. Ten meals make a juvenile, thirty make an adult strong enough to wear equipment.

3 · Explore

Lift the fog, one hex at a time

One great continent of fern meadows, forests, swamps, river valleys, coasts, deserts and volcanoes, shared by every nest. Explore it yourself or send your dinosaurs, and bring home what they find.

4 · Fight and forge

Beat the wild, forge your gear

Raptors stalk the forests and old tyrants guard the volcano. Win to bring home bones, feathers, obsidian and rare eggs, then forge weapons and armor no shop sells.

5 · Breed

Breed the next legend

Pair an adult male and female of the same species. Their egg inherits their genes, a little better or worse, so every generation can be stronger than the last.
