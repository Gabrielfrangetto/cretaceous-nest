Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Cretaceous Nest is a free browser game. This page explains what data the game keeps, why, and how you can see or delete it. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

Your account and your game

Playing needs a free account. Your progress (eggs, dinosaurs, coins, inventory and map) is kept on our server, tied to your account, and is not stored in your browser. The browser only remembers small preferences, such as the last tab you opened and your water reminder settings.

What we keep

For your account we keep:

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time link (or the email of the Google account you sign in with)
  • A display name, only if you choose one
  • Your game progress, so you can continue on any device
  • Technical sign-in records, such as when you last signed in, kept by our authentication provider for security

We use this data only to run the game and your account. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who stores the data

Accounts and saves are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The website is hosted by Render. Fonts load from Google Fonts. These providers may process data on servers outside Brazil, under their own security and privacy commitments.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your email, display name and saved progress from our database right away.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data at any time. Open Sign in → Account in the game to change your display name or delete your account. For anything else, such as a copy of your data, write to the contact below.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. Younger players need permission from a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Contact

Questions about privacy: gfrangetto@hotmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell signed-in players in the game.
