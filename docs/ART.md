# Art rules for dinosaur drawings

Every prompt for a new or edited dinosaur drawing follows these.

## Realism
- Living animal, not a skeleton: no hole or opening in the skull shows through the skin.
  - No sunken snout: the nasal opening (ceratopsians) and the antorbital fenestra (theropods) are
    covered by the same continuous scales as the rest of the face, almost flush, no smooth patch,
    no outline around it. The fleshy nostril is small, at the front of the snout.
  - No sunken frill: a ceratopsian's frill is a fairly flat plate under continuous scales, its rim a
    little thicker; no hollow, no outline of the openings in its bone.
- No movie myths. Theropods have lips over their teeth. No spikes, horns or crests the fossils
  don't show.
- A baby looks the same whatever its sex.

## Prompt lines to include
```
No sunken snout: the side of the snout between the eye and the nostril/beak is covered by the same
continuous scales as the rest of the face, almost flush, no smooth patch, no outline around it.
No sunken frill (ceratopsians): the frill is a fairly flat plate under continuous scales, only its
rim slightly thicker, no hollow, no outline of an inner opening.
```

## Making the sprite
- Side view facing right, full body, pure flat white background (#FFFFFF), no ground shadow.
- Then the same image with ONLY the background changed to pure flat black (#000000), from the white
  one, so both match pixel for pixel: the sprite is cut out from the pair (soft edge on any
  background).
